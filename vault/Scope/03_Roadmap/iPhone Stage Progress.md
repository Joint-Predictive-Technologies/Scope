## Ahead of Projected Schedule

The original SCOPE_IPHONE15_VISION.md assumed iPhone 1 = July 2026 (now).
RULE_CLUSTER and morning brief (iPhone 5) are already live. Outcome
tracking (iPhone 8) is in calibration. This is 6–12 months ahead of plan.

## What's Blocking iPhone 8 Progress

The reasoning layer (regime recognition, historical analogues, structural
permanence) depends on having enough resolved outcomes to calibrate against.
Currently: 324 labeled, ~2,687 still waiting on their +20d window.

**Priority:** Let calibration clock run. Don't rush feature work when data
maturation is the real lever.
