01
Raw data ingested
Scope continuously monitors political, regulatory, corporate, market, and geopolitical sources — congressional disclosures, SEC Form 4 filings, the Federal Register, Senate lobbying records, foreign-lobbying filings, FEC campaign finance, USPTO patents, USASpending.gov contracts, Polymarket odds, Reddit, geopolitical OSINT, and corporate earnings calls — on source-specific schedules from 15 minutes to daily.

02
Rules score every signal
14 detection rules analyze each data source for significant patterns. Signals are scored by severity, recency, and rule weight. When four or more independent rules fire on the same ticker within 24 hours, RULE_10 generates an AI-written analyst narrative.
Signal score = sev × 0.5^(h/48)
× rule_multiplier
RULE_10 multiplier: 2.0×
RULE_06 multiplier: 1.5×
