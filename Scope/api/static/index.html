Signal Architecture
Eight rules. One picture.
RULE_01B
First Touch
Fires when a member opens a brand-new position — no prior disclosed history in that name.
● Live
RULE_02
Congressional Clusters
Fires when 3+ members trade the same ticker within a 7-day window.
● Live
RULE_06
Insider Trades
Executive Form 4 trades 2× or more above the insider's historical average.
● Live
RULE_07
Polymarket
Politically-linked prediction markets moving more than 10pp in 24 hours.
● Live
RULE_08
Federal Register
New proposed regulations containing sector keywords mapped to tracked equities.
● Live
RULE_09
Lobbying Spikes
Companies with quarterly lobbying spend up more than 50% year-over-year.
● Live
RULE_11
Gov Contracts
$50M+ federal contracts awarded to publicly traded companies via USASpending.gov.
● Live
RULE_ANOMALY
Attention Spike
Fires when a ticker receives 2.5× its normal signal rate in 30 days. Also flags first-ever appearances in Scope data.
● Live
RULE_REDDIT
Reddit Signal
High-upvote posts in investing subreddits that mention tracked tickers alongside political keywords.
● Live
RULE_OSINT
Geopolitical OSINT
Conflict and humanitarian events from GDELT Project and ReliefWeb mapped to market exposure via region→sector→ticker logic. Updates every 15 minutes.
● Live
RULE_10 — Marquee Signal
Cross-Source Corroboration
Fires when 2+ independent rules converge on the same ticker within 48 hours. Generates a plain-English analyst narrative via LLM explaining why the convergence matters for investors.
