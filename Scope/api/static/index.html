Platform Guide
How Scope Works
01
Raw data ingested
Scope monitors 14 data sources — congressional disclosures, SEC Form 4 filings, Federal Register proposals, Senate lobbying records, FARA foreign filings, FEC campaign finance, USPTO patents, USASpending.gov contracts, Polymarket odds, Reddit, geopolitical OSINT, and corporate earnings calls — on source-specific schedules from 15 minutes to daily.
✓ RULE_01B fired — $RKLB
✓ RULE_06 fired — $RKLB insider sell
✓ RULE_08 + RULE_09 fired — $RKLB
✓ 4 rules converge → RULE_10 queued
02
Rules score every signal
14 detection rules analyze each data source for significant patterns. Signals are scored by severity, recency, and rule weight. When four or more independent rules fire on the same ticker within 24 hours, RULE_10 generates an AI-written analyst narrative.
Signal score = sev × 0.5^(h/48)
× rule_multiplier
RULE_10 multiplier: 2.0×
RULE_06 multiplier: 1.5×
03
You're positioned early
Signals surface before earnings announcements, before they receive broad market coverage, and ahead of retail attention. The Daily Brief synthesizes overnight intelligence into actionable context. Use the /ask terminal to query the full alert database conversationally.
