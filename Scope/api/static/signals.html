Everything moving on this page is a real Scope alert. All three components subscribe to one shared event bus fed from /alerts; none has a data path of its own and none has a simulator. In the graph below, an edge is a claim — a line is drawn only where a stored field says the two things are related, never from co-occurrence. Scope has no WebSocket or SSE endpoint, so this is polling, not push — new alerts appear at the next poll, not instantly. If the poll fails, the helix stops gaining rungs and the pulse goes quiet: that is the honest reading of "we don't know", and nothing here will invent activity to fill it. And if a backlog is larger than one drain can carry, the status line below says BACKLOG TRUNCATED rather than reporting "live" over an incomplete picture.
